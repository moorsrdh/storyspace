Acotar

A Court of Thorns and Roses (2015) A Court of Mist and Fury (2016) A Court of Wings and Ruin (2017) A Court of Frost and Starlight (2018)

Feyre Archeron is nineteen, starving, and keeping her family alive through the winter by hunting in the woods — until she kills a wolf that turns out to be a fae in disguise. Taken across the wall into Prythian as punishment, she is brought to the manor of Tamlin, a High Lord of the Spring Court.

  • Shaped roughly like the island of Great Britain, this high-stakes Fae realm is divided into seven distinct territories called Courts. Four of these are governed by the permanent cycle of the seasons—Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter—while the other three reflect specific times of day—Dawn, Day, and Night. Each region is ruled by an immensely powerful, nearly immortal High Lord who commands unique elemental magic tied directly to their land. Separated from the mortal human world by a massive, invisible magical Wall, Prythian is a beautifully dangerous land filled with political intrigue, ancient wars, and diverse magical creatures ranging from elite winged warriors to deceptive nature spirits.

  • The Spring CourtThe Spring Court is a land of eternal renewal and blooming flora, ruled by the fiercely protective High Lord Tamlin. Tied deeply to the earth, the magic of this court revolves around nature manipulation and advanced shapeshifting. Tamlin commands the ability to control plant life and transform himself and others into predatory beasts, using his raw, wild power to guard his borders and keep the peace in his blossom-scented realm.

  • The Night CourtThe Night Court is the largest, most mountainous territory in Prythian,ruled by the fiercely loyal High Lord Rhysand. The court's magic is a potent blend of darkness manipulation, starlight, and shadow-wielding, but its most feared asset is Rhysand's Daemati power. As a Daemati, he can effortlessly enter, read, alter, or mentally destroy an enemy's mind from afar, keeping the hidden paradise city of Velaris safe behind a veil of shadows and starlight..

  • The Summer CourtThe Summer Court is a tropical coastal territory of sunlit waters and coral palaces, ruled by the progressive and fair High Lord Tarquin. The magic here is deeply bound to the ocean, granting its ruler complete mastery over water manipulation and tidal control. Tarquin can command sea currents, summon massive waves, and manipulate moisture itself, making his court a formidable naval power with defenses as fluid and unpredictable as the sea

  • The Winter Court is a majestic, frozen expanse of jagged glaciers and snow-covered mountains, ruled by the resilient High Lord Kallias. The land's magic gives its ruler absolute authority over ice, frost, and sub-zero temperatures. Kallias can manipulate ice to construct massive fortresses, weaponize freezing winds, and generate blinding blizzards, using the harsh, beautiful elements of his eternal winter to shield his people.

The Kindred's Curse Saga

Spark of the Everflame (Book 1) Glow of the Everflame (Book 2) Heat of the Everflame(Book 3) Burn of the Everflame (Book 4)

The Kindred’s Curse Saga follows Diem Bellator, a mortal healer in a world ruled by cruel, magical demigods called the Descended. When her mother mysteriously vanishes, Diem leaves her safe village to find her, uncovering dangerous secrets about her own family and hidden magical powers.To survive, she joins a mortal rebellion and forms a tense, slow-burn alliance with a powerful Descended prince named Luther. Caught between two warring worlds, Diem must master her new magic to stop an ancient evil and save her people.

  • Lumnos (The Realm of Light and Shadow)Lumnos is a majestic and culturally wealthy realm famously known as a sanctuary for artists, artisans, and sophisticated high society. At the start of the saga, it is under the tyrannical rule of the dying King Ulther (with his shadow-wielding heir, Prince Luther Corbois, heavily shaping the realm's politics). The Descended of this realm possess high-tier mental and light-and-shadow manipulation magic, commanding ethereal elements to weave illusions, construct breathtaking marble cities, and enforce strict class divides. Their royal lineage is tightly bound to the "Crown of Lumnos" and guarded by ferocious, telepathically linked flying creatures called gryverns

  • Fortos is a stark, militaristic realm that serves as the primary training ground and home for Emarion’s elite armies. It is governed by a ruthless Descended King who enforces absolute discipline and heavy restrictions on the mortal populace. Marked by their striking, blood-red eyes, the Descended of Fortos wield a terrifying dual magic centered on biological control: the absolute ability to either heal or kill through rapid decay. Uniquely, this power divides by gender, as females traditionally inherit the gift of healing while males possess the lethal power of decay. This hyper-focused focus on physical supremacy makes Fortos the most aggressive and physically dominating kingdom on the continent.

  • AFaunos is a savage, untamed territory dominated by dense wilderness, vast animal kingdoms, and ruthless apex predators. It is governed by a Descended Crown who rules by a brutal "survival of the fittest" ethos, rejecting the gilded politics of Lumnos in favor of raw strength. The magic inherent to the Descended of Faunos centers entirely on beast manipulation, hyper-heightened physical senses, and total control over the animal kingdom. The inhabitants live strictly by the same violent codes as the creatures they command, making Faunos an incredibly dangerous and unpredictable region to traverse.

  • AThe mortal villages are impoverished, insular communities scattered across the lesser-valued lands of Emarion, such as the remote areas where the protagonist, Diem Bellator, grows up. Completely devoid of the divine magic of the Descended, these villages are defined by hard labor, strict curfews, and survival-driven industries like healing, farming, and blacksmithing. They do not have magical lords; instead, they are overseen by mortal town leaders or oppressive Descended governors who treat the non-magical populace as second-class citizens. Despite their lack of elemental powers, these villages harbor deeply hidden ancient secrets, a legacy tied to the mythical Everflame, and the seeds of a massive underground rebellion.

Rebecca Yarros

The Empyrean Series

Fourth Wing (2023) Iron Flame (2023) Onyx Storm (2025)

Violet Sorrengail was supposed to enter the Scribes Quadrant — quiet, safe, surrounded by books. Instead her mother, a general, forces her onto the parapet walk into the Riders Quadrant, where the odds of surviving to graduation are brutally low.

  • The setting of most of Fourth Wing and Iron Flame, Basgiath sits on high cliffs and is divided into four quadrants: Riders, Scribes, Healers, and Infantry. Violet's entire first year is a gauntlet — the parapet walk on day one can kill you before you've even met a dragon. The college is also sitting on top of the kingdom's darkest secret: the wards that protect Navarre are failing, and the leadership has known for years. Basgiath is a place of brutal hierarchy, forbidden alliances, and the ever-present threat of the venin beyond the walls.

  • Home to the bonded riders of Basgiath, the Riders Quadrant is the most prestigious and most lethal section of the college. Life here runs on a strict dominance hierarchy — senior riders set the rules and violence between cadets is largely tolerated. Violet navigates this as someone physically small in a world that respects strength above all. Her dorm, the flight field, the sparring rings, and the hatching grounds are all within the quadrant — and so is Xaden Riorson, wingleader and the son of a rebel her mother executed.

  • he kingdom that frames the entire series — prosperous, orderly, and built on lies. Navarre's rulers have maintained the illusion of safety by controlling what citizens know about the venin threat and the cost of keeping the wards alive. As the series progresses, Violet's discoveries pull back the curtain on just how deliberately the Senatus has kept its people ignorant. Navarre is both home and the central antagonist in a structural sense.

  • The largest and most rugged province in southwest Navarre, bordered by the sheer Cliffs of Dralor. It was the birthplace of the failed Great Rebellion. Its ancient capital, Aretia, houses the fortified Riorson House.

  • The Vale is the sovereign, highly guarded valley hidden directly behind the cliffs of the Basgiath War College in Navarre. Serving as the ancestral home, hatching grounds, and nursery for all dragons on the continent, this massive, bowl-shaped forested wilderness is completely self-governed by the dragon leadership council, known as the Empyrean. Humans are strictly banned from entering the territory on pain of death, with the sole exception of the annual Threshing ritual where military cadets enter the open fields to be judged by the beasts. The valley holds immense geopolitical importance because its collective dragon magic directly powers Navarre's massive Wardstone, projecting an invisible, magical shield across the country that protects the kingdom from enemy invasion.
